About Corey Abercrombie

Local guidance with a personal approach

Corey Abercrombie is a REALTOR® serving Lexington, Columbia, Lake Murray, West Columbia, and communities throughout the South Carolina Midlands. He grew up in West Columbia and now calls Lexington home.

As a veteran with 12 years of service in the Army National Guard, Corey understands the demands that military service and Permanent Change of Station moves can place on individuals and families. He helps military members, veterans, and their families manage the home buying or selling process with responsive communication and practical guidance.

Corey's background also includes more than 20 years of experience in construction and engineering, along with over a decade of real estate investing. That experience informs the questions he asks when clients evaluate a property's condition, possible improvements, and long-term considerations.

Whether a client is buying a first home, preparing for a PCS move, selling a property, relocating to the Midlands, or evaluating an investment, Corey's goal is to make the process clear and straightforward.
