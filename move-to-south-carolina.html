Moving to South Carolina

Moving to South Carolina? Start With a Local Buyer's Agent

Our team helps relocating buyers from every state find the right community and the right home, with remote tours and a clear plan from the first call through closing.

What you get when you work with us

  • Help choosing the area first. We compare communities around your work, commute, budget, schools, and lifestyle before you look at houses.
  • Remote tours. Live video walkthroughs and neighborhood drive-throughs so your in-person trip is focused on the best options.
  • Agents across the state. Columbia and the Midlands, Lexington and Lake Murray, Blythewood, Rock Hill and York County, and Myrtle Beach.
  • Coordination on both ends. If you are selling where you live now, we can connect you with a Coldwell Banker agent there and line up the timing.
  • A local closing team. Lenders, inspectors, and South Carolina closing attorneys coordinated for you while you are still out of state.
