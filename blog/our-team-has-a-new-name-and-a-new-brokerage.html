Exciting Real Estate News: Our Team Has a New Name and a New Brokerage

Say hello to The Tyler Simmons Team at Coldwell Banker Realty!

July 8, 2025 · 4 min read · The Tyler Simmons Team

Originally published in The Simmons Newsletter on July 8, 2025.

Big things are happening around here, and we’re finally letting the secret out! After years of serving amazing clients across South Carolina with passion and purpose, we’re stepping into a bold new chapter. Say hello to The Tyler Simmons Team at Coldwell Banker Realty!

This exciting move to Coldwell Banker Realty in Columbia, SC gives our team the tools, technology, and national reach to better serve buyers and sellers throughout the Palmetto State.

Why the change?

Partnering with Coldwell Banker means we’re leveling up.
✅ More marketing power
✅ More resources for YOU
✅ A brand with global recognition and local roots
✅ Unmatched exposure for your listings

At the end of the day, this move is all about creating even better results and experiences for our clients. Whether you're relocating to Columbia, investing in Myrtle Beach, buying in Lexington, or selling in Chapin, we’ve got the local know-how and the national backing to guide you home.

What stays the same?

💙 Our heart for South Carolina
💙 Our full-service commitment to clients
💙 Our incredible team (still here and growing)
💙 Our drive to help you love where you live

We’re the same dedicated team you know and trust, now with a name that reflects our future: The Tyler Simmons Team. Expect more service, more innovation, and a whole lot more fun.

What’s new?

✨ Fresh new look
✨ Advanced marketing and listing tools
✨ Access to Coldwell Banker’s powerful global network
✨ Stronger support for buyers and sellers across the entire state

Our mission remains unchanged: to provide opportunities, build community, and deliver superior service that turns dreams into reality.

Ready to buy or sell a home in South Carolina?

Let’s talk. We serve Columbia, Chapin, Rock Hill, Lexington, Myrtle Beach, and everywhere in between. From first-time buyers to luxury listings, we’ve got the experience, strategy, and passion to make your move successful.

We’re just getting started. Follow along as The Tyler Simmons Team at Coldwell Banker Realty grows, evolves, and brings a fresh energy to South Carolina real estate.

Let’s do something amazing together 💙
